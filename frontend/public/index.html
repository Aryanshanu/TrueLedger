The problem

Financial data proves everything on its own. Nobody proves it against everything else.

A borrower's bank statement, GST filings, and investment records usually get read in isolation - by different reviewers, at different times, rarely cross-checked against each other before a decision ships. That gap is exactly where risk hides: GST officers uncovered ₹15,851 crore in fake input-tax-credit claims across 3,558 shell firms in a single quarter - the kind of bank-vs-GST mismatch a cross-check catches automatically and a siloed review can miss entirely.

The infrastructure to fix this already exists and is scaling fast: India's Account Aggregator network has crossed 500 million fulfilled consents across 1,100+ regulated entities and 600+ crore data fetches, enabling ₹3.82 lakh crore in lending in FY26 alone. But every one of those consents expires - and at that scale, nobody is tracking by hand which borrower's consent is about to lapse mid-decision.

What this system does about it

Three specialist agents read bank, GST, and investment/insurance data independently; an orchestrator cross-checks their findings against each other by rule, not by hoping a reviewer remembers to; and a borrower's confidence score decays automatically as their consent runs out, instead of silently staying valid past its window. Every claim carries a citation back to the exact transaction or filing period it came from - the decision is auditable the moment it's made, not reconstructed after a complaint.

Business case

Built for where lending is actually headed.

AA-enabled lending already accounts for 8.4% of retail and MSME lending by value and 11.8% by volume in India (FY26) - and Sahamati's own roadmap names GST, CBDT, and EPFO as the next data sources joining the network. A pipeline built to cross-check bank data against GST data today is positioned to cross-check against those sources the moment they land, with no architecture change - each new FI type is just another specialist agent reading from the same consent-aware model this system already runs on.

Where this goes next

Wiring this same pipeline to a live Account Aggregator sandbox once that regulatory path is cleared; extending the rule table (agents/rules.py) as real underwriting edge cases surface; and turning the Desk's book-level view into a true portfolio risk dashboard - the same cross-checking discipline applied across a lender's entire book, not just one borrower at a time.

Credits

A credit decision is a fiduciary act. Every number here is traceable to a source, every confidence score is earned - never assumed - and a stale consent costs exactly what the math says it costs. That discipline is the product.
